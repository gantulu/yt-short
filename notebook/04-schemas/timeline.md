# Unified Timeline Contract — V1

Visual and audio observations share one chronological timeline.

## Visual timeline
Capture scene boundaries, actions, camera changes, environment changes, and other material visual events with timestamps.

## Audio timeline
Audio categories: narration, dialogue, music, sfx, ambient, unknown.

## Rules
- Preserve temporal order.
- Keep visual and audio timestamps on the same time base.
- Do not infer exact dialogue without sufficient audio evidence.
- Dedicated transcription may be used when dialogue or narration verification is required.
- Gaps or uncertain timestamps remain explicit for downstream validation.
